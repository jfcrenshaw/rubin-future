Rubin Future is a lightweight public hub for the community effort to imagine, debate, and document what should happen with Rubin Observatory after the LSST baseline survey ends.

The site is intentionally simple: meetings live in one data file, documents live in another, and the narrative pages can be edited directly in Markdown.
