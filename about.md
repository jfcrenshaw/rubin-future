---
title: About
lede: Why this site exists and what it should help the Rubin community coordinate.
---

The Legacy Survey of Space and Time will define Rubin Observatory's first decade of science operations. Rubin Future is a place to organize community thinking about what comes next.

This site should help the community:

- preserve a public record of meetings and workshops;
- collect planning documents and reference materials;
- make participation pathways easy to find;
- keep the effort understandable to newcomers.

The site is intentionally small and static so that updates can happen through ordinary GitHub edits.
